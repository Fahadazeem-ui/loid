03 PRIVACY, MADE PLAIN

Your words aren't
the product.

Loid only sees what you send through its commands. It doesn't listen in on the server, read a channel backlog, or pull in files behind your back.

Read the setup notes ↗
01

Private is the default.

AI responses are visible only to you unless you actively choose a public reply.

✓
02

Memory fades away.

Chat context stays in RAM, expires after two hours, and clears when Loid restarts.

✓
03

You decide what to send.

Only text you submit (plus short-lived /ask context on follow-ups) goes to Anthropic.

✓
04

Clear it when you want.

Use /clear to remove Loid's local memory. It cannot retract text already sent to Anthropic.
